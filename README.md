One sentence from a house cat, five seconds after sweeping a glass off the table: the glass was leaning over the human's laptop like it owned the place, so the cat relocated it to the floor — and now it expects gratitude.

> That glass was leaning over your laptop like it owned the place, so I relocated it to the floor — you're welcome, and the laptop sends its thanks.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes the excuse to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, checked first against the competition gate: 1 to 200 characters of UTF-8 text, exactly one sentence, a cat's defence with no apology and no confession.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
